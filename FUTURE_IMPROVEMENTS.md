# Future Improvement Suggestions

Here are some ideas for future enhancements to the Car Cost Estimator:

## 1. More Granular Maintenance Costs
- Allow users to specify different categories of maintenance (e.g., tires, oil changes, major repairs).
- Provide suggested maintenance costs based on the specific car model and age.

## 2. Insurance Estimation
- Integrate with insurance API or provide a simplified estimation tool based on user profile and car type.

## 3. Fuel/Energy Cost Estimation
- Add parameters for average annual mileage and fuel/electricity prices.
- Calculate fuel costs based on the car's efficiency (liters/100km or kWh/100km).

## 4. Multi-Car Comparison
- Allow users to compare multiple cars side-by-side in terms of total ownership cost and break-even points.

## 5. Tax and Registration Fees
- Include regional taxes (e.g., "bollo" in Italy) and transfer of ownership fees in the initial purchase cost.

## 6. Historical Price Trend Visualization
- Improve charts to show more historical data and trends for specific models.

## 7. User Accounts and Saved Simulations
- Allow users to create accounts to save their simulations and track changes over time.

## 8. Export Reports
- Option to export the cost analysis and charts as a PDF report.
